Danger Zone - Semester Management
Promote / Catch Back Students

Execute semester shifts for all registered **Students**. Please note that **Admins** remain static in their classrooms.

Operation Rules:
  • **Promotion**: Shifts students to the next semester. If the target classroom doesn't exist, the system returns an error.
  • **Graduation**: Students in their final semester (based on the course semester limit) will have their accounts **permanently deleted**.
  • **Catchback**: Shifts students back to their previous semester (minimum Semester 1). Requires previous semester classroom to exist.
  • *Admins and Notes are not moved.*
